## Stack

## Users

Two audiences, weighted equally (confirmed):
- Founders / product managers looking for a designer for a contract or freelance project (SaaS, CRM, dashboards, internal tools, mobile apps).
- Hiring managers at product companies evaluating Artem for a full-time Product / UI-UX Designer role.

Both arrive from a link (Telegram, email, job application, Behance) and decide within a minute whether to open a case or write.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio homepage for **Артем Мута** (Artem Muta), Product / UI-UX designer. It replaces a Notion page as the public front door: show specialization, five key projects, experience, and a direct way to get in touch. Success = visitor opens a case study or writes via email / Telegram.

## Positioning

Designs complex digital products — SaaS platforms, fintech tools, catalogs, multi-step flows — where interface usability directly affects users' speed and business efficiency. Full cycle: research and scenarios → information architecture → wireframes/prototypes → UI → design system → handoff. Independent product-design practice since 2025; web design (sites, landings, e-commerce) since 2018.

## Product Principles

1. The work leads — project imagery gets the largest real estate on the page.
2. Every case is stated as task → what was done, in the user's own words.
3. Contact is one click away from anywhere on the page.
4. Résumé facts (experience, education, languages) stay complete but subordinate to the work.
5. Nothing claimed that the Notion page does not say.
